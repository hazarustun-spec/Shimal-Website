Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Zoo Merge: Rescue the Animals ("the App") is made by Shimal / Hazar Üstün ("we", "us"). This page explains what data the App collects, why, and how to reach us about it.

Data controller: Hazar Üstün
Contact: hello@shimal.app

1. No account, no personal profile

The App does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal identifier to play. Your progress — score, rescued animals, coins, diamonds and settings — is stored only on your own device.

2. Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (rewarded video ads you choose to watch, and occasional interstitial ads between runs). To do this, AdMob and its partners may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), where you have granted tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • General device and app information used to serve and measure ads (device type, OS version, coarse location inferred from IP, ad interaction events)

You control this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device. Declining tracking still lets you play the App and watch ads — they are simply less personalized. For details on how Google's advertising products handle data, see Google's Partner Sites policy.

3. Purchases

Diamond packs and Remove Ads are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit (In-App Purchase). We never see or store your payment details — Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

4. What we do not collect

  • No account registration, no email, no phone number
  • No user-generated content, no chat, no social features
  • No location tracking beyond the coarse, IP-based signal AdMob uses for ad delivery
  • No sale of personal data, ever

5. On-device diagnostics

The App keeps a small set of counters on your own device (sessions played, runs completed, best score) so that, if you choose to, you can copy them from Settings and send them to us as a bug report. This never leaves your device unless you send it yourself.

6. Data retention

Gameplay data lives on your device for as long as the App is installed. Deleting the App deletes this data. Advertising identifiers are governed by Apple's and Google's own retention rules, not ours.

7. Children's privacy

The App is rated for a general audience and does not knowingly target children under 13 with personalized advertising; where App Tracking Transparency is declined or unavailable, ads are shown in a non-personalized form.

8. Your rights

Since we hold no account or personal profile tied to you, most data-subject requests (access, correction, deletion) are best exercised through your device's own privacy controls — Apple's Ask App Not to Track, and clearing the App's local storage by deleting it. For anything else, or for a resident of the EU/UK/Turkey exercising GDPR/KVKK rights over advertising identifiers, contact us below and we will route your request to the relevant advertising partner.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The current version always lives at this address; material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@shimal.app
